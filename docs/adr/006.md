# 006 — Zsh prompt telemetry owned by dev-workstation

Status — proposed

Keep the upstream Agnoster theme unmodified. A versioned Zsh fragment, loaded after Oh My Zsh, adds one ISO-style local date/time on the left and the preceding command's elapsed wall-clock time on the right when the duration is at least one second. Native Zsh extended history records timestamps and durations.

The opt-in installer previews by default and requires `--apply`, verifies backups before changing existing files, and refuses to migrate pre-existing custom Agnoster timestamp themes or legacy command-timer sources automatically. Users revert those customizations manually first, then apply this fragment. Preserve unrelated shell settings and do not use elevated privileges.

The fragment is currently macOS Zsh-specific (using `zsh/datetime`) but can be used on other Zsh hosts. Platform adapters do not own prompt behavior. A later Ansible dotfiles role may invoke equivalent opt-in behavior after its safety contract is implemented.
